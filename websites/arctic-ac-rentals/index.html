How it works

Booked to cool in one afternoon

Book

Call or send the form by noon. We confirm the unit, your outlet and your window type.

Delivery

A tech arrives in your 4-hour window. Delivery is free within 15 miles of our Kearny Mesa yard.

Setup

Pickup

Text us when you're done. We collect the unit and you only pay for the days you kept it.
