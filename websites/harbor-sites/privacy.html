Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version: we only collect what you type into our forms and the photos you send us, we use it to build and run your website, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

This policy covers harborsites.org and the information you send us through it. Harbor Sites is run by Riley Mulholland, doing business as Harbor Sites.

1. What we collect

  • The questionnaire: your business name, trade, city and service area, your name, phone number and email, links to your Google, Facebook, Instagram or Yelp pages, what you’d like the website to do, style preferences, and any logo or photos you upload.
  • The Launch Kit: your legal business name, the name, title, email and phone of the person signing up, business hours, your current website, domain and email provider details, setup call times, any photos you upload, and whether we may show your site in our portfolio or quote your Google reviews.
  • When you email or call us: whatever you choose to tell us.
  • Automatically, when you send a form: your IP address, used only to stop spam. Our rate limit keeps it for about 20 minutes. Cloudflare’s spam check (Turnstile) also looks at technical signals from your browser to tell people from bots.

We don’t use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. While you fill in the questionnaire, your browser saves your answers on your own device so you don’t lose them if the page reloads. That copy never leaves your device and is cleared once you send the form.

2. How we use it

  • To build your free sample homepage and send it to you.
  • To reply to you, set up your website, domain and email, and run the monthly plan.
  • To send you an email confirming we received your form.
  • To bill you through Stripe if you sign up.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

3. Who handles it for us

We use a few service providers to run the site. Each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, runs the form, and provides the spam check.
  • Resend delivers form submissions and confirmation emails.
  • Google runs the inbox where submissions arrive (Gmail) and serves the fonts on our pages, which means your browser connects to Google when a page loads.
  • Stripe handles payments. We never see or store your card number.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. Public business listings

To find businesses that might want a website, we look at public business listings, such as Google Maps. We may contact a business using the phone number or address it has listed publicly. If you’d rather we didn’t contact you, tell us and we’ll take you off our list.

5. How long we keep it

We keep form submissions and the emails about them while we’re working with you and for 12 months after our last contact, then delete them. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

6. Your choices

  • Email us to get a copy of the information we have about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We’ll reply within 30 days.
  • If you’re a client, deleting some information (like billing records) may have to wait until the law lets us.
  • Your browser may send a “Do Not Track” signal. Because we don’t track you across other websites, it doesn’t change anything here.

7. Keeping it safe

The site only runs over a secure connection (HTTPS), and the accounts that hold your information are protected with two-step login. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that affects your information, we’ll tell you.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you’ve already sent us, we’ll email you first.
