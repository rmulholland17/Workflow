Pricing and payment

What does it cost?

Your sample homepage is free. If you go ahead, it's $500 to build your site. The $59 a month care plan for hosting, updates and small edits is optional. That's our founding client rate. See pricing.

What's included in the $500?

Up to 5 pages built from your questionnaire and the sample, 2 rounds of changes before launch, a working contact or quote form, phone-first design, Google setup, and setup on your domain.

What does the $59 a month cover?

It's optional. It covers hosting, security certificates and updates, form emails, and small edits each month like new hours, prices, photos or text. It starts 14 days after you sign up, so you're not paying while we build.

Do I need the monthly plan?

No. You can buy the website on its own. At launch we set it up on hosting in your own account and hand it over. You can add the plan later if you want us to look after it.

How do I pay?

Once you've seen your sample and want to go ahead, we email you a secure checkout link for the website on its own or with the monthly plan. Payments are handled by Stripe, and you agree to our service terms there. Card or bank account both work.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel the monthly plan any time with 30 days' notice. Your domain stays yours, and we hand over your site files if the build fee was paid.

Is there a refund?

If you cancel before we start the full build, we refund the build fee in full. After that, it's non-refundable.

How it works

How long does it take?

The sample homepage takes a few days. The full site usually follows within a couple of weeks, depending on how quickly we get your photos and details.

What happens after I send the questionnaire?

You get a confirmation email right away. We research your business, build your sample homepage and send you a private link to it within a few days.

What if I don't like the sample?

Tell us what's off and we'll take another pass, or walk away. There's no charge either way.

Do I have to write the content?

No. The questionnaire asks the right questions, and we write the page copy from your answers, your reviews and your listing. You review it before anything goes live.

Do you work with businesses anywhere?

Yes. Everything happens online: the questionnaire, the sample, reviews and launch. It doesn't matter where your business is or what kind of business it is.

Your website

Can you match a style or brand I already have?

Yes. Send your logo, colors, or links to sites you like, and we design around them. If you're not sure what you want, we'll suggest a few directions.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. Your domain stays in your name, and once the site is paid for, it's yours, files and all.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Small edits are part of the monthly plan. Bigger changes, like new pages, are quoted first. The site is plain, standard files, so you can also hand it to any other web developer.

Switching from an old site

I already have a website. Will I go offline when we switch?

No. Your old site stays up until the new one is ready and approved.

Will my email keep working?

Yes. We copy your email settings before changing anything, so messages keep arriving during and after the switch.
