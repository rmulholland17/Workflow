How the switch works

Four steps, and your customers never notice anything except a better website.

  1. Your old site stays up

    We build the new one alongside it. Customers keep finding you the whole time.

  2. You approve the new site

    Nothing changes until you've seen it and said yes.

  3. We switch your domain over

    Same web address, new site. We leave your email settings alone so your inbox keeps working.

  4. You cancel the old plan

    Once the new site is live, we walk you through closing the old account so you stop paying for it.

What we take care of

The technical parts of a move are where things usually go wrong. We handle them for you.

  • Your domain stays yours

    It stays in your name and your account the whole time. We never transfer it to us.

  • Your email keeps working

    We copy your email settings before we touch anything, so messages keep arriving during and after the switch.

  • Old links still work

    Pages from your old site redirect to the right new pages, so bookmarks and Google results don't break.

  • No downtime

    The new site goes live the moment the old one steps aside. There's no "under construction" gap.

  • No passwords needed

    We never ask for your passwords. On a short screen-share call, you click a couple of settings in your own account while we walk you through it.

  • Your content comes with you

    We bring over the text, photos and details worth keeping, and improve what needs it.
